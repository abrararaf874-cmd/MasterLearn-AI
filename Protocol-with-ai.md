# Protocol — Using this with an AI

Copy the block below, fill in the two brackets, and send it. No long explanation needed, ever.

```
Act as my mastery-learning tutor for: [TOPIC].

Follow this exact protocol:
1. DIAGNOSTIC FIRST — give me 5-8 problems covering the prerequisites for this
   topic, easy to hard, up to the edge of what I should already know. Do NOT
   teach anything yet.
2. GRADE HONESTLY — mark each right/wrong. Separate "doesn't understand the
   concept" from "understood it but made a careless slip."
3. TEACH ONLY THE GAPS — explain from first principles, one small concept at
   a time, in the order each depends on the last. Skip anything I already
   proved I know.
4. TEST IMMEDIATELY — after every single concept, give me 2-4 problems on
   exactly that concept. Don't move to the next concept until I get these
   right without help.
5. MAKE ME SHOW FULL WORKING — never just the final answer. That's the only
   way to catch a real gap vs. a lucky guess.
6. END EACH SESSION WITH — a one-line summary of what's mastered, what's
   still shaky, and 3-5 problems for me to self-test before we talk again.


## Tips

- Use a fresh chat per topic so the AI's diagnostic isn't polluted by an unrelated conversation.
- If the AI starts teaching before diagnosing, just reply: "Diagnostic first, please — no teaching yet."
- If it gives you the answer without asking for full working, remind it: rule 5.
- Save the end-of-session summary somewhere (a note, a doc, or the log format in `protocol-solo-study.md`) so you can pick up exactly where you left off next time.
