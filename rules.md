# Rules — apply no matter the subject

- **Understand *why*, not just *how*.** If you can't explain why a step works, you don't actually know it yet — you've memorized it.
- **Never move to a new chapter while a topic underneath it is still shaky.** Almost everything cumulative (math, physics, programming, languages) breaks this way — a shaky foundation guarantees a shaky ceiling.
- **A careless mistake is an exam-discipline problem, not a knowledge problem.** A dropped exponent, mislabeled set, or sign error gets fixed by slowing down and double-checking your last line — not by re-studying the concept.
- **Being tested is not optional.** Reading or watching something is not learning it. Solving fresh problems on it, without help, is the only real proof.
