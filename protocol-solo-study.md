# Protocol — Studying Alone (no AI, no Google)

Same four-step loop as the AI version, done by yourself with a textbook or problem set.

## The loop

1. **Diagnostic** — before opening a new chapter, attempt the chapter's end-of-chapter problems cold, with no reading first. See exactly what you get wrong.
2. **Study only the gap** — read or watch only the section covering what you got wrong. Don't re-read the whole chapter if most of it is fine.
3. **Test immediately** — solve fresh problems on that exact sub-topic (different numbers, same idea) right after studying it. Don't wait.
4. **Explain it out loud (Feynman Technique)** — teach the concept as if to a 15-year-old, out loud or on paper, using no jargon you can't unpack in plain words. Where you get stuck or start hand-waving — that's your real gap. Go back to step 2 for that piece only.

## Spaced revisit schedule

For anything you've solved cold once, come back to a few fresh problems on it at **day 1, day 3, day 7, day 21**. Not a full re-read — just enough problems to check it's still solid.

## Self-grading rubric

Write this next to each topic in your log:

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ | Correct + clean method → mastered |
| ⚠️ | Correct but slow / roundabout → needs more reps |
| ❌ | Wrong → not mastered, back to step 2 |

## Weekly log format

Keep one line per topic per week:

```
Matrices: mastered | Vectors: shaky (row reduction) | Circle: not started
```

A ready-to-copy log template is in [`log-template.md`](./log-template.md).
